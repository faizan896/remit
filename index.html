How we estimate these numbers

The exchange rate is the live mid-market rate — the real rate currencies trade at. Fiat rows marked quoted are real provider quotes for your amount; rows marked estimate are our own researched assumptions. Either way this is a guide, not an offer — confirm with each provider before sending.

Fiat quotes

For corridors it covers, we load each provider's fee and exchange rate for your exact amount from Wise's public comparison service and show the date each quote was collected. Keep in mind that Wise is one of the providers being compared, quotes can be a few days old, and some reflect new-customer promotions. When a provider has quotes for several sending countries we use the middle one. If the service has no data for a corridor, we fall back to the estimates below.

The formula

received = (amount − fee) × midRate × (1 − margin%)
lost = amount × midRate − received

Fiat estimates (used only when no quotes are available)

  • Wise — 0% FX margin + ~0.5% fee (it genuinely uses the mid-market rate)
  • Remitly — ~1.5% margin + $1.99
  • Western Union — ~2% margin + $5
  • PayPal / Xoom — ~3.5% margin + $4.99
  • Bank wire — ~4% margin + $30

Crypto (USDT) assumptions

USDT is pegged ~1:1 to USD, so a transfer tracks the real cross-rate. Cost = the chain's network fee (Plasma ~free; Solana, Arbitrum, Monad sub-cent — Monad uses USDC, as it has no USDT; Tron ≈ $2; Ethereum ≈ $1.50–15) plus two costs you can adjust in the calculator: the cost to buy the stablecoin with your money (default 1%) and an off-ramp spread to cash out into local currency (default 0.5%). Set both to your corridor's real exchange/P2P rates to see the honest comparison.

Heads up Crypto isn't risk-free. Off-ramp spreads in restricted corridors can be far higher than 0.5%, and USDT involves exchange/P2P trust, KYC, edge volatility, and a legal status that varies by country. Check what's legal and available where you are before relying on it.

Domestic mode

Domestic transfers have no exchange rate, so the only cost is the fee. For each of 21 countries we list the main local rails (instant payment, bank transfer, mobile wallet) with a typical or regulated sender fee, and compare them with buying a stablecoin, sending it, and cashing out in the same currency. All domestic fees are estimates: fees differ by bank, account type and monthly free allowances, and no live feed exists. Each row shows the basis for its fee. Cash-out agent fees are only included where the row says so.
